# NarrativeLab 1.3.6

Spreadsheet concurrency safeguards, dependable embedded corkboard history, and smoother floating sticky notes.

## Spreadsheet data safety

- Spreadsheet tabs commit the active Univer editor and wait for pending mutations before switching views or closing.
- Sparse teardown snapshots can no longer silently remove persisted worksheets, rows, or columns; explicit worksheet and axis deletion remains supported.
- Concurrent Excel or Univer writes are detected before commit. NarrativeLab keeps the canonical workbook and preserves the rejected editor state under `System/Spreadsheet Recovery`.

## Whiteboard and sticky notes

- Embedded corkboard Canvas now receives Ctrl/Cmd+Z, Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z, and Ctrl/Cmd+Y through the correct Canvas history stack, even though it is hosted in a detached workspace leaf.
- Text fields, CodeMirror editors, and spreadsheet cell editors retain their own undo history instead of being intercepted by the corkboard.
- Floating sticky notes apply their palette before asynchronous file loading and remain transparent across hover, focus, and active states, preventing the first-hover white flash.

## Compatibility

Requires Obsidian 1.12.7 or later. Installation artifacts remain `main.js`, `manifest.json`, and `styles.css`.
